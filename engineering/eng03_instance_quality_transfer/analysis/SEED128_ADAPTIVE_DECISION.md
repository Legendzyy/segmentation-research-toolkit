# D0/M0 paired seed128 readout and adaptive decision

2026-09-26 16:04 Asia/Shanghai. Blueberry development validation, full254 images / 1803 GT, input640, scheduled last-epoch weights and the same independent mask evaluator. D0 and M0 completed 60 epochs at seeds 42 and 128. Seed42 was used to select M0 for replication; seed128 is the new paired replication. These are not external or test results.

The small-AP benefit that motivated the M0 third seed **reversed** on seed128. Both seeds have positive AP90 and negative AP75 contrasts, but the total AP contrast is only −.0126/+ .0638 pp; two selected/development seeds cannot establish a general AP gain or a quality trade-off. Seed128 M0 also has fewer detections in the complete low-threshold queue (6945 versus 7563), though this count alone cannot explain AP. The score-.25 coverage loss is 1/3 GT respectively and is descriptive, not an AP decomposition.

The common-GT analysis gives no evidence of uniformly improved small-instance masks. For 109/108 aligned common small GT in seeds42/128, mean M0−D0 mask IoU is −.00299/−.00331. Overall aligned common GT IoU is −.00041/−.00085, despite positive AP90. Conditional mask quality and the complete ranked prediction set therefore tell different stories; score ranking, unmatched detections, and mask-IoU threshold crossings remain competing explanations. The thin metric uses a raster skeleton proxy, not a semantic thin-branch annotation.

A CPU-only replay of the saved full254 predictions (`SMALL_AP_THRESHOLD_DIAGNOSTIC.json`) localizes the reversal. There are 132 COCO-small GT. At mask IoU .90, seed42 D0→M0 small AP changes .2209→.3138 and maximum-detection small-GT recall 49→54; seed128 changes .3349→.2752 and recall 58→50. At IoU .50, small AP modestly increases in both seeds (.7814→.8112 and .7725→.7825), while seed128 recall decreases 131→129. The high-IoU small tail is particularly unstable. These counts and area-specific AP are diagnostic summaries of the full ranked predictions, not a causal AP decomposition; the 132 GT also limit subgroup precision.

The pre-evaluation rule in `ADAPTIVE_SEED_POLICY.md` released M0_s2024 only if seed128 AP loss was no worse than −.20 pp **and** small AP gained at least +.50 pp. The AP clause passed (+.0638 pp); the small-AP clause failed (−.2780 pp). The barrier recorded `STOP` at 16:03:40. M0_s2024 has an explicit `SKIPPED_BY_ADAPTIVE_SEED_POLICY` receipt with **0 trained epochs**. D0_s2024 completed its already-running 60 epochs unchanged. The queue terminal label is a process-completion label, not evidence that four models trained. The paired package trained three new models, not four.

Research implication: retain direct-output D0 as a simple comparator, but do not present selective-mask M0 as a reproducible small-object improvement. The AP90 pattern warrants a limited failure analysis of ranking, matched versus unmatched predictions and threshold crossings before revising supervision. A future revision should be frozen as a new development version and initially use one seed under the adaptive policy. It should be compared to a strong native/feature-KD budget bridge; it must not be chosen by retroactively treating AP90 as the original primary endpoint. No third M0 seed is justified by the registered scheduling rule.

Sources: `SEED128_PAIRED_EVALUATION.json`, the four full254 `evaluation/*/ap.json` and PASS receipts, `M0_S2024_EVALUATION_BARRIER.json`, and `runs/*/completion.json`. No test access; shared GPU training/evaluation time is non-isolated.

The already completed D0_s2024 checkpoint was independently evaluated after the barrier: AP .844379, AP75 .895112, AP90 .745004, small AP .622610, full254 PASS. This is a third **D0 baseline** training seed, not a third paired M0 contrast. Across D0 seeds 42/128/2024, AP spans .839156–.844379. The observed spread is a reminder that sub-tenth-point M0−D0 overall contrasts from two seeds are not a robust superiority finding.

The subsequent saved-prediction, per-GT COCO .90 match audit (`SMALL_GT_MATCH_TRANSITIONS.json` and `.csv`) identifies a concrete endpoint change. Among the same 132 small GT, seed42 has 39 matched by both models, 10 by D0 only and 15 by M0 only: net +5 M0 high-IoU matches. Seed128 has 46 both, 12 D0 only and 4 M0 only: net −8. This agrees with the independent maximum-detection recall counts and shows the seed reversal is not merely an AP precision interpolation artifact. For the GT matched by both, mean M0−D0 matched detection score is nearly zero at seed42 and +.0263 at seed128; a uniform decrease in scores of surviving high-IoU TPs is not supported. Changes in which candidate crosses mask IoU .90, missed GT, false positives and full score ordering still need separation. Class 1 supplies 8 of 15 M0-only GT at seed42 but only 1 of 4 at seed128. These are diagnostic counts on a 132-GT subgroup, not independent proof of class-specific behavior.
