# Results draft — ENG03 Blueberry development evidence

All formal ENG03 student models used the fixed full254/1803-GT input640 independent mask evaluator on the scheduled last checkpoint; AP values are 0–1 and differences are percentage points. No test set was opened. Historical ENG02 A0 native n AP .840904, A2 teacher s AP .847758 and K1 feature KD AP .842336 provide context, but their training provenance is not a matched multiseed contrast to ENG03.

The plain GT-linked response KD D0 reached .842408 in the development-selected seed42, .839156 in seed128 and .844379 in seed2024. Same-budget native N0 achieved .840163 in seed128 and .842095 in seed2024. D0−N0 was −.1007 and +.2284 pp, respectively (descriptive mean +.0639 pp); the sign reversal does not support a stable full AP advantage. In seed128, D0's score-.25 same-class box coverage exceeded N0 by 12 GT and its aligned common-GT mask IoU by .000695, while AP fell .1007 pp and AP90 fell 1.1638 pp. This is a descriptive discrepancy, not a causal AP decomposition. Figure `analysis/PAIRED_NATIVE_D0.png` displays both paired seeds and subgroup trade-offs.

Q0's task-dependent teacher-advantage selection attained .841307 against D0 .842408 in seed42; the complementary C0 factorial cell reached .838899. M0's seed42 small AP exceeded D0 by 1.8292 pp at −.0126 pp overall AP, but seed128 small AP was .2780 pp lower, triggering the registered zero-epoch M0_s2024 skip. A ranked TAL-positive mask-anchor variant R0 was .4062 pp below D0 overall in its single seed2024. The simple additive response-plus-feature F0 was .3751 pp below D0, .1467 pp below native N0 and .1783 pp below feature K1 in the matched seed2024. Therefore neither selection, anchor change nor simple feature addition establishes a distinct beneficial full-prediction method here.

The shared-GPU completed formal training processes sum to 37.601 hours across 12 complete 60-epoch jobs, with two interrupted partial attempts additionally counted against the budget. The first R0 and K1 attempts were preserved; successful reruns began from the same frozen initialization and seed, not a claimed exact checkpoint continuation. Mature nearest-method adaptation, external MinneApple training/development, P1D M1 same-budget bridge, isolated latency and final test were **not performed**. This is a PAUSE-B research-stage readout, not an affirmative efficacy result or a submission-ready comparison.
