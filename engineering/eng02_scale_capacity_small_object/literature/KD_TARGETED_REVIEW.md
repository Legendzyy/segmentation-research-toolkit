# ENG02 KD targeted literature audit (2026-09-25)

This is a branch-specific addition to ENG01's 20 technical and five engineering-journal neighbors; those historical assets are not copied or modified. Original conference proceedings, publisher pages, and the author's preprint are linked below. The ENG02 result does **not** establish that KD is ineffective in general: it tests only a frozen YOLO11n-seg student, YOLO11s-seg teacher, a fixed 5%-scale feature loss, and the two specified P3/P4/P5 feature objectives on Blueberry seed42/full254. No test split was accessed.

**Position.** The primary literature establishes foreground weighting, feature alignment, localization/output transfer, scale-aware KD, and instance-mask distillation. ENG02 therefore makes no method-novelty claim for K1/K2. The frozen comparison instead asks an engineering question: whether a larger native YOLO11s-seg teacher's observed capacity headroom can be transferred to a same-size native YOLO11n-seg student under two simple, controlled losses. On this seed and dataset, it cannot meet the prespecified qualification threshold. Journal gains differ in task, denominator, model, and resource conditions; none licenses changing ENG02's gate after seeing its result.
