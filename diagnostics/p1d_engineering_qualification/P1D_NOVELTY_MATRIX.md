# P1D post-training specialization novelty matrix

Audit date: 2026-09-19. Thirty-two primary papers / official implementations, including the 25 source-screened works inherited from the immutable P1C audit and seven newly examined refinement/staged-training neighbors. This is a targeted audit, not an exhaustive novelty proof. `Unverified` means the inspected source does not establish the field; it must not be interpreted as `No`. An abstract-level screen cannot rule out an implementation hidden in a supplement. Dataset entries are examples explicitly established by the audit, not necessarily exhaustive. No external benchmark test images or outcomes are used for choosing this experiment's method.

The inherited evidence depth is retained in `artifacts/audit/prior_documents/OBJECTIVE_NOVELTY_MATRIX.md`. New search evidence is in `artifacts/literature/`. ## Specific novelty answers and claim limits

1. **Training a detector, freezing it and optimizing only segmentation is already explicit in DETR's official recipe.** SharpContour also reports independent refinement training with the original model frozen. This broad claim is blocked.
2. **An exact YOLO/YOLACT coefficient-only residual calibration match has not been established by the inspected sources.** That is an unresolved search result, not proof of priority. YOLACT, PatchDCT and compact dynamic-mask literature are necessary nearest-neighbor checks.
3. **The inspected nearest refinement methods use spatial features, image/mask inputs, or new representations.** This leaves coefficient-only input as a possible specific engineering distinction, conditional on results, without licensing “first coefficient refinement.”
4. **High-resolution mask supervision is established and is already a native implementation knob.** Combining it with a small residual is not automatically a novel mechanism. M3 must beat both single components under the registered comparisons.
5. **Post-training specialization as a general method family is established.** P1D can at most support a narrowly scoped, efficient specialization recipe with strong-baseline, equal-budget, three-seed and external-dataset evidence.

The [pinned YOLACT loss](https://github.com/dbolya/yolact/blob/902073d414728280b496c5f0a59a8e8285b3c13c/layers/modules/multibox_loss.py) and [configuration](https://github.com/dbolya/yolact/blob/902073d414728280b496c5f0a59a8e8285b3c13c/data/config.py) also contain predicted-box cropping and coefficient gating options. Their existence does not establish a coefficient-only residual, nor improvement under this protocol; it blocks broad claims that these neighboring ingredients are new.

**NOVELTY_RISK = HIGH.** Nearest staging prior: DETR. Nearest frozen-base spatial-refinement evidence: SharpContour / BPR. Nearest representation prior: YOLACT, with PatchDCT relevant to global compact-vector correction. Current engineering novelty and publication qualification remain **UNRESOLVED** until the registered experiments finish. This audit supplies no reason to add a new module.
