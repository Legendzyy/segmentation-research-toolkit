## Claim boundary

The closest prior for assignment is maIoU/GmaIoU and mask-aware query matching; for ranking it is Mask Scoring R-CNN and Rank & Sort; for global compact-vector coupling it is PatchDCT; for local correction it is PointRend, BPR, SharpContour and Mask Transfiner; for spatial coefficients it is BlendMask/SipMask. P1E therefore cannot claim that score–mask mismatch, mask-aware assignment, spatial coefficients, boundary refinement, local residuals or thin-detail preservation are new. Any remaining contribution would need a sharply scoped empirical bottleneck and a mechanism materially different from these neighbors.

**NOVELTY_RISK = HIGH.**
