# EXP-SIS-20260921-P1E frozen protocol

This protocol is frozen before any P1E candidate, oracle or local-control outcome is inspected. P1A=E, P1B=A+STOP, P1C=F/PIVOT and P1D=H/STOP/`COEFFICIENT-LINE-CLOSED` remain immutable.

## Primary model and data

Use P1D `BASE60_s42/weights/last.pt`: seed42 was the preregistered development seed and epoch60 the frozen strong horizon. This choice is based on protocol role, not P1E outcomes. Do not retrain it or select another P1D seed after viewing results. Use 64 validation images selected deterministically by the lowest `SHA256("20260921:" + key)` within the existing low/medium/high count strata, with quotas 21/21/22. Read no test asset.

## Candidate sets

For every GT, SET-A is every raw anchor whose best class equals the GT class and whose decoded box IoU to that GT is at least 0.30. SET-B is every official TaskAlignedAssigner positive whose `target_gt_idx` is that GT when the frozen validation GT is passed through the official detached assignment path. SET-C is SET-A with matching-class confidence at least 0.001 before NMS. SET-D is the final class-aware NMS output, greedily one-to-one matched to GT by box IoU at least 0.50, exactly as the inherited evaluator. A GT is relevant for the primary candidate-gap gate only if SET-D exists and SET-A contains at least two candidates; detection absence is reported separately.

The source anchor identity is the raw 8,400-point index. Candidate masks use that anchor's frozen coefficient, the image's frozen prototype bank and its own decoded predicted box. SET-D survival is read from NMS `return_idxs`; no mask is regenerated by a learned component.

## Subgroups

COCO input-grid area groups are small `<32^2`, medium `[32^2,96^2)`, large `>=96^2`. Thin pixels inherit P1C/P1D: Zhang skeleton pixels with Euclidean distance to GT background `<=2` input pixels; thin is present with at least three such pixels. Close-neighbor means the minimum GT-boundary distance to another GT is `<=8` input pixels. High-curvature is omitted because a stable inherited definition does not exist.

## Candidate verdict

`CANDIDATE_MASK_ALIGNMENT=STRONG` if at least 20% of relevant GT have SET-A mask-IoU gap `>=0.03`, or a preregistered small/thin/close subgroup with at least 30 GT reaches 30%. It is `MODERATE` if the overall fraction is at least 10% or mean gap is at least 0.01. Otherwise it is `WEAK`. Boundary and thin gaps are reported independently and cannot substitute for the mask-IoU gate without a named subgroup meeting the same repetition rule.

`MASK_SCORE_ALIGNMENT=MISALIGNED` requires the candidate gate to be STRONG and clustered median Spearman(det-score, mask-IoU) below 0.30. `PARTIAL` covers a STRONG/MODERATE gap without that correlation condition; otherwise `ALIGNED`.

## Prototype oracle

For SET-D candidates, fit the existing 32 global coefficients with the numerically audited P1A float64 standardized L-BFGS BCE contract. Fit separately on GT-raster-box support and predicted-box support. Evaluate native and both oracle masks on interior (`distance to boundary >4`), boundary (`<=4`), inherited thin skeleton, boundary F and full IoU.

`THIN_PROTOTYPE_CAPACITY=AVAILABLE` if GT-support oracle median thin recall is at least 0.95 and at least 80% of thin instances have residual failure at most 0.10. It is `SUPPORT_LIMITED` if GT-support median is at least 0.95 and the median GT-minus-predicted-support thin-recall gap is at least 0.10. It is `LOCALLY_LIMITED` if median GT-support thin recall is below 0.90, median interior recall is at least 0.98 and at least 20% of thin instances retain failure at least 0.10. Other outcomes are `INCONCLUSIVE`.

## Local-control oracle

O0 is the native selected-candidate mask. O1 is the stable predicted-support global coefficient oracle. O2 changes only wrong-sign logits inside a frozen GT boundary band, clipped to predicted-box support, by the minimum residual needed to cross zero. Bands are 2/4/8 pixels; width4 is primary. A full predicted-support pixel oracle is a trivial ceiling. Five deterministic random pixel sets with the same number of controllable pixels are controls. Fractions 25/50/75/100% define the privileged correction frontier; pixels with smallest absolute logit are corrected first. This is a ceiling, never a deployable method.

`GLOBAL_LOCAL_CONTROL=COUPLING-SUPPORTED` only if width4 O2 improves thin recall over O1 by at least 0.05 without reducing O1's FP improvement, at least 20% of thin instances repeat a gain of 0.05, and the median gain exceeds the random-control median by at least 0.03. A median gain of at least 0.02 or repetition of at least 10% is `COUPLING-WEAK`; otherwise `NO-COUPLING-EVIDENCE`.

## Fault taxonomy

Use mutually exclusive precedence F→E→A→C→D→B: F detection absence; E GT-support versus predicted-support IoU gap `>=0.05` or thin gap `>=0.10`; A candidate mask gap `>=0.03`; C local width4 thin gain `>=0.05` with no FP loss; D GT-support thin residual `>=0.10` with interior recall `>=0.98`; B GT-support global IoU gain `>=0.03`; otherwise UNRESOLVED. This precedence avoids counting one GT in several headline proportions; the joint table retains every continuous measure.

## Decision

No model training occurs in P1E. GO is permitted only for a STRONG candidate result, `LOCALLY_LIMITED`, or `COUPLING-SUPPORTED`. If several qualify, choose the branch with the largest preregistered repeated-instance margin and write one P1F blueprint. Otherwise PIVOT or STOP according to the final evidence. No result-dependent threshold change is allowed.
