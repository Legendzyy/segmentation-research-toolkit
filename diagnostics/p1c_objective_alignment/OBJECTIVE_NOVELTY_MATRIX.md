## Nearest implementation risk

The [official YOLACT loss](https://github.com/dbolya/yolact/blob/902073d414728280b496c5f0a59a8e8285b3c13c/layers/modules/multibox_loss.py#L550) already selects decoded predicted boxes when `mask_proto_crop_with_pred_box` is enabled, then assembles/crops masks and applies BCE. Its [config](https://github.com/dbolya/yolact/blob/902073d414728280b496c5f0a59a8e8285b3c13c/data/config.py) also exposes coefficient gating, prototype-as-feature inputs, target downsampling and loss normalization options. Thus predicted-box mask training, coefficient gates, and prototype readback cannot be claimed as new concepts. YOLACT's default remains GT cropping; the existence of an option is not evidence that it improves this dataset or that its resolution/order equals P1C. Pinned copies and hashes should be consulted for stable source anchors.

## Answers to the five novelty checks

1. An identical frozen-YOLO11/native640/BCE recipe has not been established by this targeted search. Closely overlapping ingredients are already in official YOLO and YOLACT. Exact sequential implementation alone is insufficient novelty because linear combination commutes with interpolation in real arithmetic.
2. Training-only zero-inference-cost improvements already exist: boundary agreement, YOLACT semantic auxiliary training, loss/ranking changes and Mask2Former optimization ablations.
3. Resolution and representation quality have extensive precedent (YOLACT, DCT-Mask, PointRend, RefineMask). The pinned YOLO loss directly supports mask_ratio1/2. No claim that this search found an exhaustive YOLO mask_ratio-only benchmark.
4. Boundary/scale metric mismatch and mask-score/AP mismatch have direct prior explanations (Boundary IoU, Mask Scoring, Rank & Sort). They do not prove a universal high-quality-tail regression in P1B; that must be measured.
5. Reliability-selective spatial refinement and globally coupled vector correction have direct neighbors (SegFix, PointRend, Mask Transfiner, PatchDCT). A coefficient identity gate would need independent predictive evidence and cannot be framed as a new quality-estimation concept.

**NOVELTY_RISK = HIGH.** Only a specific, causally isolated mismatch plus reliable deployment-quality benefit could justify further investigation. No novelty credit for ratio1, high-resolution targets, BCE crop changes, zero overhead alone, or gradient cosine diagnostics. No literature finding overrides the empirical gates.
